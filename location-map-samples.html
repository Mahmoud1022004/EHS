On the actual map.

A real map of the Cairo–Sharqia corridor with both locations plotted at their true coordinates, animated on top. The map is a self-hosted image built from OpenStreetMap, so nothing loads from a third party at runtime and there is no cookie prompt. Click a panel to replay.

Preview in: Tell me a number and I will wire it in

How the pins are placed. Both points are geocoded, not eyeballed — the office to El-Abour Buildings on Salah Salem Street, the factory to 10th of Ramadan City — then converted to pixel positions with the same projection the map tiles use. Two things worth knowing:

  • The factory pin sits on the city, not the individual plot in Industrial Zone A2 — that address is not in the open map data. At this zoom the difference is invisible, but it is an approximation and you should know it is one.
  • The map never mirrors in Arabic, only the layout around it does. Flipping it would put Cairo east of the factory.
No distance or drive time is stated anywhere. The map credits OpenStreetMap, which its licence requires.
