EHS — Egyptian Hospital Supplies

An Egyptian medical manufacturer, built on practical engineering.

Founded in 1988, EHS designs and manufactures medical supplies and healthcare support products — combining practical healthcare engineering, consistent manufacturing and human comfort.

From its Cairo office and its factory in 10th of Ramadan City, EHS develops products with clear specifications, made to serve healthcare professionals and everyday users alike.
