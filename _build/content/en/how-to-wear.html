Putting on compression stockings, step by step.

A correctly applied stocking feels smooth, even and supportive. These steps make application easier — especially the first few times.

  1. Put them on early in the day

    Legs are least swollen in the morning, which makes application easier and the fit more accurate.

  2. Reach inside and grip the heel

    Slide your hand into the stocking and pinch the heel pocket from the inside.

  3. Turn the leg section inside out

    Holding the heel, pull the stocking back over your hand so the foot section is ready and the leg section is folded back.

  4. Fit the foot first

    Slip your foot in and position the heel exactly in the heel pocket, with the toe section sitting comfortably.

  5. Roll the stocking up gradually

    Unroll it up your leg in short sections — don’t pull hard from the cuff.

  6. Smooth out every wrinkle

    Work out folds and creases with your palms until the fabric lies evenly from toe to cuff.

  7. Position the cuff below the knee

    The cuff should sit flat about two finger-widths below the knee crease. Never fold or roll the cuff down.

  8. Check your comfort

    The stocking should feel firm and supportive. If you feel pain, numbness or tingling, remove it and consult a healthcare professional.

Helpful tips

  • {{ICON:check}}Dry skin makes application easier — apply moisturiser at night, not right before.
  • {{ICON:check}}Rubber gloves improve grip and protect the fabric from nails and jewellery.
  • {{ICON:check}}Remove stockings before sleeping unless your healthcare professional advises otherwise.
Caring for your stockings

Keep the compression working.

Wash gently

Frequent gentle washing helps the fabric recover its shape and keeps compression consistent.

Dry away from heat

Air-dry flat, away from direct sunlight, radiators and tumble-drying heat.
