MedPress · Size & measurement guide

Find the size that fits your legs.

Compression works best when the size is right. Three simple measurements are all you need to choose your MedPress size.

Step 1

Measure in the morning.

Measure early in the day, before your legs swell, with the tape snug but not tight against the skin.

cB — Ankle

Measure around the narrowest point of your ankle, just above the ankle bone.

cC — Calf

Measure around the widest part of your calf.

cG — Thigh

For thigh-length styles: measure around the widest part of your thigh.

Step 2

Match your measurements to the chart.

Between two sizes?

The ankle measurement (cB) matters most for graduated compression. If your measurements fall across two sizes, follow the advice of your healthcare professional or pharmacist.

Short and long lengths

Short and long sizes are available upon special orders — see the leg-length chart above.

Medical guidance Product selection, compression level and usage should follow the recommendation of a qualified healthcare professional. Size ranges shown are taken from the current packaging and remain subject to confirmation by EHS.
Step 3

Check the fit.

  • Smooth from toe to cuffNo wrinkles, folds or bunching — especially at the ankle.
  • Cuff sits flat below the kneeIt should hold without digging in or rolling down.
  • Firm but comfortableCompression should feel supportive — not painful or numbing.

Your pharmacist or healthcare professional can measure you and recommend the right size and compression level.
