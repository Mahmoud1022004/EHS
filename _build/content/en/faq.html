Everything you commonly ask about compression stockings — sizing, wearing, care and where to get professional guidance.

What are medical compression stockings? {{ICON:plus}}

Medical compression stockings are knitted garments designed to apply graduated pressure to the leg — typically strongest at the ankle and decreasing up the leg. MedPress stockings are designed to provide this graduated, comfortable support for everyday wear.

Who are compression stockings recommended for? {{ICON:plus}}

The current MedPress packaging lists indications including chronic venous insufficiency, varicose and spider veins, post-thrombotic syndrome, edema and leg swelling, post-surgical and post-sclerotherapy support, lymphedema management, prolonged standing or sitting, travel-related circulation support and pregnancy-related leg discomfort.

These are indications, not guaranteed outcomes. Product selection, compression level and usage should follow the recommendation of a qualified healthcare professional.

Do I need to consult a doctor before using them? {{ICON:plus}}

Compression is a medical product, and the right level and fit depend on your situation. Consult a healthcare professional before use — especially if you have diabetes, circulatory disorders or another condition affecting leg health.

How do I find my size? {{ICON:plus}}

Measure your ankle (cB), calf (cC) and — for thigh styles — thigh (cG) circumference in the morning, then match your measurements to the MedPress size chart, which runs from S to XXXL.

Are short and long lengths available? {{ICON:plus}}

Yes — according to the current packaging, short and long sizes are available upon special orders. Ask your supplier or contact EHS for availability.

What compression level do MedPress stockings provide? {{ICON:plus}}

MedPress is made in three compression classes: CCL I at 15–20 mmHg, CCL II at 25–30 mmHg and CCL III at 30–40 mmHg. Which class is right for you is a clinical decision — product selection, compression level and usage should follow the recommendation of a qualified healthcare professional. For documentation, use the professional enquiry page.

How do I put compression stockings on correctly? {{ICON:plus}}

Put them on early in the day, fit the heel first, roll the fabric up gradually and smooth out every wrinkle. Never fold the cuff down. Our step-by-step guide covers the full technique.

How should I wash and care for them? {{ICON:plus}}

Gentle washing and air-drying away from heat help preserve elasticity. Detailed care instructions will be published together with the confirmed material specification.
