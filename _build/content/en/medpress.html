Intended use

When compression stockings may be recommended.

According to the current MedPress packaging, medical compression stockings may be recommended for the following conditions and situations — always based on the advice of a qualified healthcare professional.

  • {{ICON:check}}Chronic venous insufficiency
  • {{ICON:check}}Varicose veins
  • {{ICON:check}}Spider veins
  • {{ICON:check}}Post-thrombotic syndrome
  • {{ICON:check}}Edema and leg swelling
  • {{ICON:check}}Post-surgical support
  • {{ICON:check}}Post-sclerotherapy support
  • {{ICON:check}}Lymphedema management
  • {{ICON:check}}Prolonged standing
  • {{ICON:check}}Prolonged sitting
  • {{ICON:check}}Travel-related circulation support
  • {{ICON:check}}Pregnancy-related leg discomfort
{{ICON:info}}
Medical guidance These are indications listed on the current product packaging — not guaranteed medical outcomes. Product selection, compression level and usage should follow the recommendation of a qualified healthcare professional.
{{ICON:alert}}
Before you use compression Consult a healthcare professional before use if you have diabetes, circulatory disorders or another condition affecting leg health.
