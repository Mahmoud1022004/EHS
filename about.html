About EHS

A modern Egyptian medical manufacturer — since 1988.

EHS — Egyptian Hospital Supplies — has designed and manufactured dependable medical supplies for professional care and everyday use for nearly four decades.

Support, engineered.

EHS combines practical medical engineering, disciplined manufacturing and a human-centred approach to create products that support clinicians, healthcare partners and people in daily life.

The company focuses on reliable medical products, practical healthcare engineering, product comfort, consistent manufacturing and clear product specifications — for healthcare professionals and everyday users alike.

Its flagship compression line, MedPress medical compression stockings, carries that idea into a range designed for graduated, comfortable leg support.

Our story

Nearly four decades of trust.

Founded in 1988, EHS has grown into one of Egypt’s established manufacturers of medical consumables and hospital supplies — serving hospitals, clinics, pharmacies and healthcare institutions every day.
