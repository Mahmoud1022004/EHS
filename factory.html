The Factory

Inside the EHS factory.

Where dependable products are made — 10 production lines, an integrated quality system and nearly four decades of manufacturing discipline in 10th of Ramadan City.

Our approach

Practical engineering, disciplined production.

EHS has manufactured at 10th of Ramadan City, Industrial Zone A2 — one of Egypt’s established industrial cities — since 1988, with an integrated facility that manages the complete process from raw-material inspection to finished-product packaging.

Our manufacturing approach is built around controlled processes, materials selected for durability and comfort, and product specifications that are stated clearly and matched in production.
