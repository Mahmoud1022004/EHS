Intended use

Listed indications.

The current packaging states that medical compression stockings may be recommended for:

  • Chronic venous insufficiency
  • Varicose veins
  • Spider veins
  • Post-thrombotic syndrome
  • Edema and leg swelling
  • Post-surgical support
  • Post-sclerotherapy support
  • Lymphedema management
  • Prolonged standing
  • Prolonged sitting
  • Travel-related circulation support
  • Pregnancy-related leg discomfort
Medical guidance These indications are taken from the existing product packaging and are not guaranteed medical outcomes. Product selection, compression level and usage should follow the recommendation of a qualified healthcare professional.
Caution Consult a healthcare professional before use if you have diabetes, circulatory disorders or another condition affecting leg health.
